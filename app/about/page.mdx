# About

I'm a full stack engineer based in Tokyo, Japan, currently working on production
reliability and infrastructure modernization.

My path here went basically like: an associate's degree in
Control Systems Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Sasebo
College (2020–2023), then a bachelor's in Computer Science and Intelligent
Systems at Oita University (2023–2025). Along the way I picked up backend and
full stack internships, Flask web app at Sigma-i for local governments' data needs, a
route-search app built on bus GTFS-RT data at MobileCreate.

## What I work on

Most of my time goes into keeping legacy production services
healthy while they get modernized. That's meant leading Spring Boot 3.x
upgrades across several services, breaking dependency changes, deprecated
API migrations, pushing test coverage to reasonable pcts, and
performance testing along the way. It's also meant spearheading the migration
of 1,600+ cron jobs off bare-metal VMs onto Kubernetes using Apache Airflow,
with logging, error propagation, and a CI/CD pipeline for DAG generation.

A good chunk of the job is also just chasing down slow things: profiling
high-traffic services, then rolling out
OpenTelemetry and performance-testing tooling once I find the bottleneck.

## Outside of work

I like building infrastructure for myself as much as for my day job. This
site runs on a small self-hosted platform I maintain on a single VPS — Caddy
in front, Zitadel for SSO, everything else in Docker. The centerpiece is
_**Marsa**_, a minimal self-hosted PaaS I built to
replace Coolify: it deploys Docker Compose apps with automatic TLS, GitHub
push-to-deploy, Zitadel-backed SSO, JWT-protected APIs, and a goroutine-based
async deploy worker pool underneath. I've also shipped smaller things like an
Android app for offline kanji lookup using the KANJIDIC2 dictionary, unimaginatively named ["WhatsThisKanji"](https://github.com/Sardonyx001/whats-this-kanji) .

Day to day I mostly reach for Java, Go, Python, and TypeScript, with Spring
Boot, React/Next.js, Docker, and Kubernetes as the usual stack. 
I speak English, French, and Arabic fluently, and Japanese well
enough to work in it day to day.
