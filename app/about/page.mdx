# About

I'm a full stack engineer based in Tokyo, Japan, currently working on production
reliability and infrastructure modernization for Rakuten Travel — Japan's #1
online travel agency, part of Rakuten Group.

My path here went through Sasebo before Tokyo: an associate's degree in
Control Systems Engineering at the National Institute of Technology, Sasebo
College (2020–2023), then a bachelor's in Computer Science and Intelligent
Systems at Oita University (2023–2025). Along the way I picked up backend and
full stack internships — a data-entry and reporting web app at Sigma-i, a
route-search app built on real-time bus GTFS-RT data at MobileCreate, and a
short Domain-Driven Design API project at Yumemi — before joining Rakuten
Group as an Application Engineer in April 2025.

## What I work on

At Rakuten, most of my time goes into keeping legacy production services
healthy while they get modernized. That's meant leading Spring Boot 3.x
upgrades across several services — breaking dependency changes, deprecated
API migrations, pushing test coverage from around 10% to over 85%, and
performance testing along the way. It's also meant spearheading the migration
of 1,600+ cron jobs off bare-metal VMs onto Kubernetes using Apache Airflow
and `KubernetesPodOperator`, including a multi-cluster POC with custom
logging, error propagation, and a CI/CD pipeline for DAG generation.

A good chunk of the job is also just chasing down slow things: profiling
high-traffic services with Jennifer APM and SQL analysis, then rolling out
OpenTelemetry and performance-testing tooling once I find the bottleneck. I
built an MCP server that gives the team live access to Jennifer APM across 51
production servers, which cut incident investigation time from hours to
minutes — and a Claude Code plugin bridging Bitbucket, Confluence, and Jira to
automate dependency investigation, now used by 30+ people across teams. I'm
also involved in containerizing and migrating 100+ legacy Spring Boot services
to Kubernetes.

## Outside of work

I like building infrastructure for myself as much as for my day job. This
site runs on a small self-hosted platform I maintain on a single VPS — Caddy
in front, Zitadel for SSO, everything else in Docker. The centerpiece is
[Marsa](https://marsa.jamell.dev), a minimal self-hosted PaaS I built to
replace Coolify: it deploys Docker Compose apps with automatic TLS, GitHub
push-to-deploy, Zitadel-backed SSO, JWT-protected APIs, and a goroutine-based
async deploy worker pool underneath. I've also shipped smaller things like an
Android app for offline kanji lookup using the KANJIDIC2 dictionary.

Day to day I mostly reach for Java, Go, Python, and TypeScript, with Spring
Boot, React/Next.js, Docker, and Kubernetes as the usual stack, backed by
Postgres, MySQL, Oracle, MongoDB, or Elasticsearch depending on what's in
front of me. I speak English, French, and Arabic fluently, and Japanese well
enough to work in it day to day.
